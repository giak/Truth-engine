# Omitted duplicate Library copies

Suffix copies such as `(1)`, `(2)` and similar historical duplicates are not systematically embedded when a canonical file or source bundle of identical role is available.

This is a de-duplication rule, not deletion of historical provenance:
- the current canonical control plane is included;
- original per-investigation source bundles are included;
- TRACELOG preserves the operational history;
- known recovery exceptions INV-139/140 are explicitly retained.

No missing terminal bytes were reconstructed.
