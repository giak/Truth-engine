# CLUSTER_ICEBERG v2.2

**Purpose:** identify material omissions and reconstruct bounded hidden reality (`Ξ`). Formula: `@PAT[ICEBERG]`; method: `forensic/REASONING.md` when `Ξ≥5`.

## Concepts

- `OMISSION_SELECTIVE`: unexplained period/geography/sample selection.
- `CATEGORY_TRICK`: category excludes relevant subgroups.
- `SHADOW_POPULATION`: unregistered/inactive/informal population absent.
- `DENOMINATOR_MANIPULATION`: base switches or remains undefined.
- `TIMEFRAME_CHERRY`: start/end chosen to alter comparison.
- `AVERAGING_TRICK`: mean hides distribution/dispersion.
- `PROXY_SUBSTITUTION`: indirect indicator presented as target measure.
- `COMPARATIVE_ABSENCE`: number lacks relevant baseline/comparator.
- `METHODOLOGY_OPACITY`: definition/formula/data access missing.
- `CONFIDENCE_MISSING`: precision shown without uncertainty.

## Query stems

`{metric} methodology exclusions denominator` | `{metric} full dataset revision history` | `{population} unregistered informal estimate` | `{metric} median distribution confidence interval` | `{claim} comparable region long series`

## Deep review/output

Apply the four forensic questions; test whether exclusions are defensible; reconstruct only comparable values; show range/sensitivity and innocent methodological explanation. Output `Ξ observations | visible definition/value | omissions | factor/NOT COMPUTABLE | sensitivity | status/gaps`.

**Pairs:** MONEY, NETWORK, TEMPORAL. Omission does not prove concealment or intent.
