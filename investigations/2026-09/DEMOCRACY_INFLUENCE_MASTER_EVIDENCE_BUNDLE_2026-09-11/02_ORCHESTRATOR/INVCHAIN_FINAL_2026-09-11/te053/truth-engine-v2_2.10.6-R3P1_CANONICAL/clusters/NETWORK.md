# CLUSTER_NETWORK v2.2

**Purpose:** construct a sourced, typed actor/control graph (`🌐`). Use `@PAT[NET]`. Co-occurrence and shared affiliation are leads, not influence or coordination.

## Concepts

- `ELITE_CLOSURE`: recurring insiders and evidenced access barriers.
- `GATEKEEPER`: node controls a relevant resource/information flow.
- `DENSITY_HIGH`: many evidenced edges under a stated graph definition.
- `CENTRALITY_CONCENTRATION`: few nodes dominate a stated metric.
- `PERIPHERY_EXCLUSION`: documented exclusion from relevant process.
- `REVOLVING_DOOR`: dated cross-sector movement.
- `INFORMATION_CONTROL`: ownership/editorial/platform control over distribution.
- `INFLUENCE_CASCADE`: sourced decision/instruction flows across levels.
- `NETWORK_ENDOGAMY`: recurring family/school/club ties in appointments.
- `INVISIBLE_BROKERS`: high-betweenness actor with low public visibility.

## Query stems

`{entity/person} board employer adviser register` | `{organization} ownership governance members` | `{decision} meeting minutes participants` | `{funding} recipient contractor` | `{network} appointment process access rules`

## Deep review/output

Define nodes/edge types/time window; attach provenance to every edge; compute metrics only on the explicit graph; compare a topology without speculative edges; connect centrality to documented action. Output `🌐 score | graph schema | edge table | metrics/NOT COMPUTABLE | gatekeepers | action links | gaps`.

**Pairs:** MONEY, WAR, BIO.
