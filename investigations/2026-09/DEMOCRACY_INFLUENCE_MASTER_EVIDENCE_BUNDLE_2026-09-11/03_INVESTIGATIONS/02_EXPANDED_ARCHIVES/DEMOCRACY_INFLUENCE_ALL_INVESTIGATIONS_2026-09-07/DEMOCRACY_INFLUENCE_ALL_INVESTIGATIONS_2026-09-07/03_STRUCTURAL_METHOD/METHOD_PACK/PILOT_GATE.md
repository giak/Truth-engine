Purpose: calibrate METHOD_PACK across five distinct evidence families before bulk execution.

Fixed pilot order:

1. `INV-010` — historical/covert archival case

3. `INV-049` — private foundation / NGO flows
4. `INV-071` — French state information-governance apparatus

Review after **each** run. Do not batch.

## Per-run gate

P0 if any:

- Truth Engine canonical gates/verifier broken by METHOD_PACK;
- corpus article treated as independent proof;
- source status used as truth rank;
- relation/proximity promoted to coordination/control without evidence;
- causality/impact leap (P0–P2 evidence used to claim P5–P7);
- predetermined systemic thesis defended;
- material contradiction suppressed;
- RENARD restarts whole run or fabricates unavailable access.

P1 if any:

- scope too broad to terminalize coherently;
- inherited corpus causes large redundant searching without delta;
- systemic object stops at components and misses material bridge/use/effect;
- rival explanation search is materially asymmetric;
- handoff cannot support next-run selection/registry update;
- new ideas are not triaged.

PASS when:

- no unresolved P0/P1 generalizable METHOD_PACK defect;
- TE final remains canonical;
- handoff is concise and sufficient;
- RENARD gate is discriminating (not automatic, not under-triggered);
- new ideas can be added/merged/deferred without parallel source of truth.

## Pilot completion gate

After pilot 5:

`METHOD_PACK_CANONICAL := YES` only if all generalizable P0/P1 defects discovered during pilots are patched and at least the affected pilot(s) replay cleanly.

Otherwise keep `MAIN_DYNAMIC` blocked.
