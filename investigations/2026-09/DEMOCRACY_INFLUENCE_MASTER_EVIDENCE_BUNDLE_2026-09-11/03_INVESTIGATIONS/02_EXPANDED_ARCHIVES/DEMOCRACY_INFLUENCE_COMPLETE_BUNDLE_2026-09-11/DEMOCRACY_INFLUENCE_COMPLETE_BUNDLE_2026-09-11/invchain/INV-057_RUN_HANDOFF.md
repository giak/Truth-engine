## Delta central

INV-057 establishes a general mechanism of mission-aligned nongovernmental intermediation without validating a generic proxy model. Public and private funders can define politically substantive objectives, finance organizations, monitor grants and obtain traceable program outputs, while several inspected architectures preserve independent boards or executive committees, applicant-defined actions, operational flexibility and explicit speech-attribution boundaries. The supported chain is funder objective/resources -> grant/agreement -> intermediary program/output, with variable autonomy and monitoring. It does not become funding -> command/tasking without authenticated instruction/control over a named operation, and program activity does not become electoral or policy causality without downstream exposure and counterfactual evidence. The strongest remaining cases are therefore actor-specific tests of tasking, governance, financial dependence and effect, not more generic proof that foreign funding exists.

## Registre causal certifié

- `public-policy objective/appropriation -> grant/agreement -> NGO/intermediary program execution` = **SUPPORTED** — Funding and mission architecture establish intermediation, not command over every act.
- `grant objective + monitoring -> specific funder command/tasking over recipient message/action` = **UNRESOLVED** — Oversight and objective alignment coexist with non-state decision rights and flexible execution.
- `funded NGO campaign/media/service -> information exposure/civic participation/intermediate institutional action` = **UNRESOLVED** — Most inspected evidence is administrative or funder-reported and lacks recipient-level counterfactual exposure measurement.
- `EED-supported Moldova pro-EU/disinformation activities -> referendum result` = **UNRESOLVED** — Activities and result co-occur, but no design estimates their marginal effect on the 50.35% Yes vote.
- `private-foundation strategy -> grant/support -> NGO advocacy -> public reform` = **UNRESOLVED** — Advocacy/support are documented; final-policy marginal causality and grantee obedience are not generally isolated.
