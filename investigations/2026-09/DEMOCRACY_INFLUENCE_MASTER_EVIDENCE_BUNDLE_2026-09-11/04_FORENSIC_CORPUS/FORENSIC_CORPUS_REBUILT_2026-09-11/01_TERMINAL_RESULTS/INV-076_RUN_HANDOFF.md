## Delta central

INV-076 closes the generic allocation and materiality mechanism of French public press support without validating a generic editorial-control model. The corpus establishes a large, multi-channel, concentrated and economically material aid architecture with substantially rule/formula-based eligibility and an explicit pluralism objective. Sector-level dependence is therefore a legitimate structural independence-risk variable, but the inspected evidence does not establish a generic subsidy -> state instruction -> editorial compliance chain, a subsidy-linked quid pro quo, or the marginal causal effect of aid on editorial line, title survival or overall pluralism. The remaining high-value tests are title-specific authenticated pressure/control chains and counterfactual aid-shock or eligibility-threshold designs, not more aggregate recipient lists.
