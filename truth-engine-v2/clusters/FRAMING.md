# CLUSTER_FRAMING v2.1

**Purpose:** deconstruct how a frame selects categories, alternatives and salience (`Λ`). A frame can be ordinary simplification; intent requires separate evidence.

## Concepts

- `OVERTON_SHIFT`: bounded change in publicly acceptable options.
- `TALKING_POINTS`: repeated distinctive phrasing with a traceable origin.
- `FALSE_DICHOTOMY`: material alternatives excluded.
- `EUPHEMISM`: language softens concrete effects.
- `LOADED_LANGUAGE`: evaluative term replaces neutral description.
- `FRAMING_BY_OMISSION`: material fact/option falls outside the frame.
- `ANCHORING`: first number/claim fixes reference point.
- `METAPHOR_MANIPULATION`: metaphor imports unstated conclusions.
- `NARRATIVE_PREEMPTION`: interpretation precedes available facts.
- `CONTEXT_STRIP`: scope/date/denominator removed.

## Diagnostic and queries

Score from documented restriction, omission, repetition and materiality [0..10]. Search: `"{phrase}" earliest/source`; `{claim} full context transcript`; `{policy} alternatives analysis`; `{number} baseline methodology`; `{metaphor} history framing`.

## Deep review/output

Reconstruct original statement and competing frames; identify excluded options; trace distinctive language; test brevity/genre constraints; map frame→audience effect separately from designer intent. Output `Λ inputs | quote map | missing context/options | origin | alternative frames | evidence/gaps`.

**Pairs:** SPECTACLE, INVERSION, OVERLOAD.
