# CLUSTER_INVERSION v2.1

**Purpose:** test reversal of action, blame, language or historical record (`Ω`, also `Κ`). Use `@PAT[GAS]` for the gaslighting subset.

## Concepts

- `ACCUSATORY_INVERSION`: accusation mirrors evidenced accuser conduct.
- `GASLIGHTING`: denial plus memory/witness attack; strict subset.
- `DOUBLETHINK`: incompatible positions maintained without resolution.
- `REALITY_DENIAL`: direct evidence rejected without adequate basis.
- `MEMORY_ERASURE`: material prior record deleted/rewritten.
- `VICTIM_FLIP`: actor/perpetrator claims victimhood; roles must be evidenced.
- `LANGUAGE_INVERSION`: operative term redefined against ordinary/legal use.
- `MORAL_INVERSION`: evaluative labels reverse while facts stay constant.
- `BLAME_REVERSAL`: responsibility shifted contrary to documented authority/action.
- `TRUTH_INVERSION`: unsupported claim labels stronger evidence false.

## Query stems

`"{statement}" archive transcript` | `{actor} prior position vote/action` | `{term} legal definition change` | `{event} responsibility inquiry judgment` | `{claim} correction rebuttal evidence`

## Deep review/output

Create before/after/action table; separate changed evidence, changed policy and contradiction; test projection with equivalent conduct and scope; distinguish legal responsibility from rhetoric. Output `Ω/Κ inputs | archive timeline | reversal type | counter-explanation | status/gaps`.

**Pairs:** GASLIGHTING, FRAMING, CONFIRMATION.
