# CLUSTER_MONEY v2.1

**Purpose:** trace material flows, ownership and conflicts (`€`). Use `@PAT[MONEY]`. Benefit and conflict are not proof of intent, corruption or causation.

## Concepts

- `LOBBY_TRACE`: disclosed/undisclosed influence contacts tied to policy.
- `SUBSIDY_SHADOW`: direct, indirect and tax support omitted from stated cost.
- `REVOLVING_DOOR`: dated movement across regulator/industry roles.
- `ASTROTURFING`: funded infrastructure behind claimed grassroots action.
- `COST_EXTERNALIZATION`: costs shifted to public, workers, environment or future.
- `CAPTURE_REGULATORY`: dependency/influence linked to regulatory decisions.
- `PROFIT_EXTRACTION`: dividends/buybacks/fees versus investment and losses.
- `MONOPOLY_HIDDEN`: common ultimate ownership obscures apparent competition.
- `BAILOUT_PATTERN`: private gain/public risk transfer across cycle.
- `FINANCIALIZATION`: essential good converted into revenue-bearing asset/debt.

## Query stems

`{entity} annual accounts beneficial owner` | `{policy} lobbying register meetings` | `{project} subsidy tax exemption contract` | `{person} interests former employer board` | `{sector} concentration external costs bailout`

## Deep review/output

Trace payer→vehicle→intermediary→ultimate beneficiary; separate declared/indirect/estimated amounts; align accounting periods; connect flow to documented decision; test legitimate service/market explanations. Output `€ inputs | flow table | ownership | COI | public/private cost | decision link | status/gaps`.

**Pairs:** NETWORK, ICEBERG, WAR, TEMPORAL.
