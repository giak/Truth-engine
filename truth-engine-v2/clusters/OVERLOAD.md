# CLUSTER_OVERLOAD v2.2

**Purpose:** test whether information volume, speed, contradiction and emotional urgency impair judgment (`Ψ`). Effects require a defined stream, window and audience.

## Concepts

- `INFO_FLOODING`: rate exceeds stated processing capacity.
- `CONTRADICTION_STORM`: incompatible claims arrive before resolution.
- `URGENCY_FABRICATION`: deadline lacks objective basis.
- `EMOTIONAL_OVERLOAD`: concurrent fear/anger/outrage dominates evidence.
- `ATTENTION_FRAGMENTATION`: rapid topic switching prevents follow-through.
- `DECISION_PARALYSIS`: overload measurably blocks choice.
- `DOOM_SCROLLING`: interface/content sustains negative consumption.
- `LEARNED_HELPLESSNESS`: repeated failed agency precedes withdrawal.
- `NARRATIVE_EXHAUSTION`: trust collapses across all accounts.
- `COGNITIVE_FATIGUE`: depleted capacity is evidenced, not presumed.

## Diagnostic and queries

Score documented volume, velocity, contradiction, urgency/emotion relative to audience capacity. Search: `{event} media volume timeline`; `{deadline} legal/operational basis`; `{claims} correction chronology`; `{platform} engagement design`; `{audience} survey behavior impact`.

## Deep review/output

Sample and count the stream; prioritize central claims; test whether real crisis complexity explains volume; distinguish exposure from effect and effect from intent. Output `Ψ inputs/window | claim queue | contradiction map | deadline basis | measured effect | cooldown priorities | gaps`.

**Pairs:** WAR, FRAMING, INVERSION.
