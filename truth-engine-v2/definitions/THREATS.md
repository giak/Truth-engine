# THREATS v2.1 — Detection and counter-checks

`@THR[]` is a triage label. Activate deeper verification only when its observable signature is present. A match does not prove a plan, perpetrator, diagnosis or intent.

## §1 Core threats

| Threat | Observable signature | Required counter-check | Defensive response |
|---|---|---|---|
| @THR[SHOCK] | acute shock + urgency + narrowed options | real deadline, prior plan, viable alternatives | pause, timeline, rights/impact trace |
| @THR[BIDERMAN] | several evidenced coercive conditions | context, duration, voluntary exit, alternative model | document conditions, restore information/contact |
| @THR[GASLIGHT] | archived contradiction + denial/pathologizing/memory attack | correction, changed evidence, quotation context | preserve archive, compare exact claims |
| @THR[INFODEMIC] | measured volume/velocity/contradiction overload | sampling window and baseline | prioritize claims, cooldown, evidence queue |
| @THR[DARK_MONEY] | decision-relevant hidden funding/ownership | disclosure rules, legal entity, audited flows | trace payer→beneficiary, expose gaps |
| @THR[REG_CAPTURE] | dependency/revolving doors + decisions favoring regulated actors | competence, public-interest rationale, enforcement record | conflict disclosure, decision chronology |
| @THR[MYTHO] | public biography conflicts with records | primary chronology, omitted negative and positive facts | biography archaeology |
| @THR[NUDGE] | default/friction asymmetry steers material choice | user intent, reversibility, informed consent | neutral defaults, friction symmetry |
| @THR[CIALDINI] | multiple influence triggers used materially | ordinary persuasion, audience agency | disclose triggers, cooling period |
| @THR[ASTRO] | grassroots claim + opaque funding + coordination evidence | genuine membership/autonomy evidence | entity/funding/infrastructure trace |

Biderman checklist: isolation, perceptual monopoly, exhaustion, threats, intermittent indulgence, omnipotence, degradation, absurd demands. Count only evidenced, applicable items.

## §3 Diagnostic indices

Use only observed, defined inputs on a documented scale. These indices prioritize review; they are not probabilities.

```text
COERCION = mean(applicable Biderman conditions)
INFODEMIC = normalized(volume,velocity,contradictions,capacity)
SOCIAL_CONTROL = mean(applicable honeypot,astroturf,controlled_opposition,surveillance,dependency,infiltration)
PSY_MANIP = mean(applicable nudge,Cialdini,emotional,digital_dark,cognitive_warfare)
```

Missing inputs remain `NOT COMPUTABLE`. Do not fill them from intuition. Threshold language: 5–6 material review, 7–8 strong indicators, 9–10 extensive indicators — never “established/total” without independent evidence.

## §4 Foundational studies — bounded use

- **Milgram:** a laboratory obedience result; 65% reached the experiment’s maximum shock setting in the best-known condition. It does not show that 65% of people obey any “fatal order”.
- **Asch:** conformity varied by condition; many participants conformed at least once, while most critical responses remained correct. Do not turn it into “75% deny their eyes”.
- **Stanford prison study:** severe methodological and interpretive disputes make it unsuitable as standalone proof that roles cause abuse.

Historical experiments can suggest questions about authority, conformity and context; they cannot establish the mechanism in the current case.

## §5 Resistance checks

For any active threat, record: independent information access, reversible choices, dissent cost, archive integrity, viable alternatives and affected-person testimony. Emergency pattern `[emotion spike + artificial urgency + single source/social pressure]` → pause, isolate claims, diversify, delay irreversible action when possible.
